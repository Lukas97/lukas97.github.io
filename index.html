My story

400+ batteries, one C# Battery Controller

In iwell's Battery Controller team I rebuild and maintain the C# Battery Controller, split out of our monolithic EMS for battery storage: setpoint execution, safety, and reporting. A Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL) over Modbus absorbs vendor-specific quirks so batteries from many manufacturers plug in. I also commission new vendor hardware on site and built Wattson (an AI troubleshooting tool over live telemetry, internal docs and logs).

Infrastructure and automation

Maintaining a fleet at scale. At iwell I commission and maintain the infrastructure which is used on the IPCs inside 400+ battery and EMS systems via Ansible. Infrastructure/Configuration-as-Code is used for deployment, support, security, and monitoring, backed by CI/CD pipelines. Before that, I brought agile ways of working to a waterfall organization, backed by Ansible automation, CI/CD pipelines, and a standardized release process.

Factory automation with C#

Four years at Prodrive Technologies automating production processes in the robotics software team, reducing cycle time and total cost of products. I integrated Visual Inspection cells and Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) into production lines, improving quality control and reducing manual production steps, and built a reporting dashboard that automated client reports, cutting manual work by 40%.
